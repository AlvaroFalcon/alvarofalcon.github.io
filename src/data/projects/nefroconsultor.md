## The problem

Primary care physicians need a quick, reliable way to assess a patient's kidney function and decide
whether to refer them to a nephrologist, following current clinical guidelines.

## What the app does

- Takes a few patient values (age, sex, plasma creatinine, albuminuria) and calculates the
  estimated glomerular filtration rate with standard formulas.
- Classifies the Chronic Kidney Disease stage and shows the prognosis classification.
- Gives guideline-based recommendations, including when to refer to a specialist.
- Includes clinical guides, references and the editorial committee.

## My role

I have collaborated on the project on and off over several years, developing and maintaining both
the native **Android (Java)** and **iOS (Objective-C)** apps.
